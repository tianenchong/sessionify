Get help

Email sessionify.support@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within two working days.

The quickest way to report a problem is from inside the app: ⋯ → Send Feedback on iPhone and iPad, or Help → Send Feedback on the Mac. It fills in your app version and device so you don't have to, and you see everything before it's sent.

Common questions

Does one subscription cover all my devices?

Yes. Sessionify Pro unlocks iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. On a new device, open Sessionify and tap Restore Purchases if Pro doesn't appear straight away.

Do people I share a session with need to pay?

No. Opening and playing a shared session is free, with no account. Recording and editing need Pro.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or App Store → your account → Subscriptions on the Mac. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Your sessions stay yours and keep opening after you cancel.

Where are my sessions stored?

As .sf files in your own iCloud Drive (or on your device), visible in the Files app. We have no server and never see them.

Why is there no transcript?

Transcription runs on your device. A very quiet recording can produce no text; check the level meter while recording. The first transcription may wait while your device downloads Apple's speech model.
