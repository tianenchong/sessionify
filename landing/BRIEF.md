# Marketing brief

**Name:** Sessionify
**Pitch:** Record the room, snap the whiteboard, mark up the handout — then play it all back in sync.
**Subtitle:** Audio, photos & notes in sync.

## Who it's for
Students, workshop and class attendees, consultants and client-meeting teams, design reviewers — anyone
whose important conversations happen in person.

## The problem
Your recording doesn't know which photo it's talking about. Your photos don't know where in the recording
they belong. Most capture tools handle only one kind of media. Filming a whole session is tiring, awkward,
and often not allowed — so Sessionify records audio and lets you capture just the moments that matter.

## Key features (as benefits)
1. **Play it back in sync** — scrub the audio and the whiteboard from that moment appears; tap a photo and the audio jumps to it.
2. **Capture without stopping** — photos, short clips, typed notes or Apple Pencil while recording; the shutter is silent so it never lands in the audio.
3. **Mark up as you go** — highlight, underline and write on PDFs and photos, each mark stamped to its moment.
4. **Transcribed on your device** — read along, tap any word to jump there, see who said what; no audio is uploaded.
5. **Follow up later** — add notes and highlights afterwards, kept separate from what happened in the room.
6. **Share the whole session** — recipients get audio, photos, notes and markup in sync; opening a shared session is free. Or export parts as ordinary files.

## Pricing
- Viewing and playing sessions: **free**.
- **Sessionify Pro: $7.99/month or $59.99/year (about 37% off)**, both with a **1-month free trial**.
- Recording, capturing, editing and transcription need Pro. One subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- Family Sharing is off at launch. Not sold in mainland China.
- Positioning: a new category — part meeting recorder, part note app. Above note apps, well below AI meeting tools, because nothing is sent to the cloud.

## Availability
Live on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sessionify/id6821321012 (one listing for iPhone, iPad and Mac).

## Platforms
iPhone, iPad, Mac (iOS/iPadOS 17+, macOS 14+). iPhone = roving capture; iPad = Pencil markup; Mac = wide-timeline review.
Android/Windows: possible future direction only — no dates, don't promise.

## Differentiators
- Works in physical rooms (meeting-notes tools only capture digital calls).
- Syncs photos, notes and PDFs to the audio in both directions — competitors sync none or handwriting only.
- Fully on-device and private; sessions live in the user's own iCloud Drive.
