## Problem strip
Your recording doesn't know which photo it's talking about. Your photos don't know where in the recording they belong. Sessionify fixes that.

## Feature sections (headline — body)
- **Scrub the audio. The whiteboard follows.** Every photo, note and highlight sits on one timeline, in step in both directions.
- **Capture without stopping.** Photos, short clips, notes and Apple Pencil while the recording rolls. The shutter is silent.
- **Mark up PDFs and photos as you go.** Highlight, underline, draw, type — each mark lands at its moment.
- **Transcribed on your device.** Tap a word to jump there. Speakers told apart. Nothing leaves your phone.
- **Add to it later.** Evening follow-ups stay separate from what happened in the room.
- **Send the whole session.** Opening it is free — no subscription needed.

## Privacy band
