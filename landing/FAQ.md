# FAQ

**Is my audio uploaded anywhere?** No. Recording, transcription and speaker identification run on your device. We never see your audio, photos, notes or transcripts, and there is no tracking. (Today the App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".)

**Where are my sessions stored?** As ordinary files in your own iCloud Drive, visible in the Files app and backed up with your device.

**Does it work offline?** Recording and transcription need no connection. iCloud sync does.

**Can I take my data with me?** Yes — audio, photos, clips and PDFs export as normal files.

**What happens when my trial ends?** You can still open and play sessions; recording, capturing and editing need Pro.

**Can someone watch a session I share without paying?** Yes, it's free to open and play.

**Does it record video?** It records audio throughout; you add photos and short clips at the moments that matter.

**Is there an AI summary?** Not built in. The transcript is made on your device and nothing is sent to the cloud. Users who want a summary can make one from the transcript with Apple Intelligence on their own device.

**Does it work with PowerPoint or Keynote?** Not yet. PDFs, photos and notes are supported.

**Which devices?** iPhone and iPad (iOS/iPadOS 17+) and Mac (macOS 14+). One subscription covers all three.

**Android or Windows?** Not today.
