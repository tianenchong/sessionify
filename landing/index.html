Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Recording and transcription need no connection, so it works in basements, on site and on planes. iCloud sync catches up when you are back online.

Does it record Zoom or Teams calls?

Sessionify is built for meetings in a physical room. It records what your device's microphone hears and doesn't join video calls.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, transcription and speaker identification run on your device. We never see your audio, photos, notes or transcripts.

What happens when my trial ends?

You can still open and play sessions. Recording, capturing and editing need Pro.

Does one subscription cover all my devices?

Yes. Pro unlocks iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Can someone watch a session I share without paying?

Yes. Opening and playing a session is free.

Where are my sessions stored?

As ordinary files in your own iCloud Drive, visible in the Files app and backed up with your device.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Audio, photos, clips and PDFs export as normal files.

Does it record video?

It records audio throughout. You add photos and short clips at the moments that matter.

Is there an AI summary?

Not built in. Sessionify gives you a full transcript made on your device, and nothing is sent to the cloud. If you want a summary and your device supports Apple Intelligence, it can write one from the transcript, also on device.

Does it work with PowerPoint or Keynote?

Not yet. PDFs, photos and notes are supported.

Android or Windows?

Not today.
