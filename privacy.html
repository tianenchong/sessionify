Privacy Policy

In short: Sessionify does not collect your data. Your recordings, photos, notes and transcripts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. We run no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and there is no account to create.

What stays on your device

Everything you capture — audio, photos, video, notes, handwriting, document markup — is stored in session files on your device and, if you use iCloud Drive, in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms. We cannot access them.

Transcription, speaker identification and text recognition run on your device using Apple's frameworks. Your audio is never sent to us or to any third party for processing.

Permissions

Sessionify asks for the microphone (to record), the camera (to add photos and video), speech recognition (to transcribe on your device) and, if you choose, your photo library (to add photos you took during a session). Each is used only for that purpose, and you can turn any of them off in Settings.

Things that leave your device only when you choose

Sharing

When you share or export a session, it goes where you send it — AirDrop, Messages, Mail and so on. Anyone you send it to can see what it contains.

Feedback

If you use Send Feedback, the app prepares an email with your message, your app version, device model and locale, and any crash reports your device has recorded for Sessionify. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself, and you can read or remove anything first. We use it only to answer you and fix problems.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details and no personal information from Apple about who you are.

Things Apple does

Your device may download speech-recognition models from Apple the first time you transcribe in a language. iCloud Drive sync and App Store purchases are provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Sessionify is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
